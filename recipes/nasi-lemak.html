Nasi Lemak

Recipe:
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 pandan leaves, tied in a knot
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups jasmine rice
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs, halved
  • 1 cup fried anchovies (ikan bilis)
  • 1 cup roasted peanuts
  • Sambal sauce (store-bought or homemade)
Instructions:
  1. In a rice cooker, combine coconut milk, water, pandan leaves, and salt. Rinse the jasmine rice and add it to the rice cooker. Cook according to the rice cooker's instructions.
  2. Once the rice is cooked, fluff it with a fork and remove the pandan leaves.
  3. To serve, place a portion of coconut rice on a plate. Arrange cucumber slices, hard-boiled egg halves, fried anchovies, and roasted peanuts around the rice. Serve with sambal sauce on the side.
